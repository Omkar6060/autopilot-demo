How do I get my review link?
  1. On your phone, open the Google Maps app and search your business name.
  2. If you manage the business: open it, tap Customers → Reviews (or "Get more reviews") and tap Share review form. Copy the link.
  3. On a computer: search your business on Google while signed in. In your Business Profile panel, click Ask for reviews and copy the link.
  4. Paste it in the box above. It usually starts with https://g.page/r/.
Where should I put the poster?
  1. Next to the billing counter, where people wait while paying.
  2. On mirrors or reception desks: anywhere customers spend 30 seconds.
  3. Post the WhatsApp status version once a week.
